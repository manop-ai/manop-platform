# Landowner and site submitter guide

## Starting capacity
Submit a site at /site-intelligence/submit (landowner, agency or developer). You can sign in so the site is tied to your account.

## What you can do now
1. Submit a site; MANOP reviews it (pending review, draft, published).
2. Open the site page for appraisal and Site Studio, and share a report by link.
3. Introductions on a landowner site are allowed by the Deal Room rules (landowner party), but no landowner dashboard screen exists yet, so conversation access for landowners is a gap to build.

## What admin does for you
Reviews the site (/admin/sites), requests evidence, publishes, and handles enquiries.
