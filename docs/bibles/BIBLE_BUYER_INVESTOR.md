# Buyer and investor guide (including diaspora buyers)

## Starting capacity
Sign up at /register, complete the short profile (budget range, where you are based, diaspora status). Buyers land on /developments; investors on /investor/dashboard.

## What you can do now
1. **Browse reviewed developments** (/developments, /development/[id]). Only developments MANOP has published appear. Missing information is labelled as missing, never filled in.
2. **Download a Buyer Pack** from a development page. Free for now. If you are signed in it is listed under Buyer Packs on your dashboard.
3. **Save developments and enquire.** Enquiries create an introduction; your dashboard shows saved developments and enquiries sent.
4. **Get Financed.** Enter your figures; MANOP builds an analysis and shows it under My Finance. Use "Compare" to put lender products side by side on your own deposit, term and price. Results say whether a product meets, does not meet or lacks information against what is recorded, with an indicative repayment. It never says which product is best or that you are approved; only the lender decides.
5. **Deal Room** for each introduction: message the developer or agency (and MANOP), see milestones, report things like a viewing or payment you have made. Your contact details stay with MANOP until it refers the introduction.
6. **Investors** also have Investment Intelligence and Site Intelligence.

## What admin does for you
- Reviews every development before you see it, and flags or requests missing information.
- Holds your introduction and refers it to the developer once your acknowledgement is on record.
- Reads your finance request, can hand it to a lender or developer, and answers in the conversation.
- Resolves disputes on milestones.

## What you should not expect
MANOP does not approve loans, give advice, verify your payments, or guarantee availability and price: confirm with the developer.
