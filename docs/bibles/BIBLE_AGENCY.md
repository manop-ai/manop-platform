# Agency guide

## Starting capacity
Register as an agency, complete /agency/onboard. Tabs: Overview, Developments, Updates, Agreement, Leads, Finance, Transactions, Get verified, Profile; Site Submission and Land & Redevelopment are linked.

## What you can do now
1. **Submit developments** you are authorised to market, stating either direct authority or a documented mandate from the developer or owner. MANOP reviews before anything is public.
2. **Leads and Deal Room** for introductions on your submissions, with conversation and milestones. Buyer details are shared only after MANOP refers.
3. **Get verified** (professional body, CAC registration, or both). Verification is reviewed by an admin; paying a subscription does not verify you.
4. **Choose a commercial route** (Agreement tab). (A) **Transaction success fee**: proposed 1.5% of the qualifying closed transaction value, paid by the agency, only when a qualifying event occurs under the applicable agreement (registering, submitting, enquiries and viewings create no fee), with a recorded initial commitment (proposed 12 months from the date you accept). (B) **Custom subscription**: there is no standard price. You send a request describing your portfolio and usage; MANOP assesses it and sends a proposal (price, duration, inclusions, which developments are covered, expiry). You accept or decline; after acceptance MANOP confirms payment and approves it before it activates. You can keep completing your profile, verification and submissions while a request is pending. All figures are proposed until MANOP approves terms. A subscription gives no exclusivity, no guaranteed commission, no verification and no confidential documents. After the commitment ends (or if MANOP authorises an earlier change) you can request a subscription; fees already triggered stay under the success-fee route.

## What admin does for you
Reviews submissions, verifies you, assesses custom subscription requests and issues proposals, approves terms, records your payment reference and activates a subscription, activates or ends your route, holds and refers introductions, records agreement execution.

## Not available yet
Route entitlements undefined; no payment gateway; cancelled-deal fee treatment and model-change notice are undecided.
