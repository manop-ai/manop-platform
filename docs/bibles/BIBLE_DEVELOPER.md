# Developer guide

## Starting capacity
Register with the developer role and complete /developer/onboard (company, cities, project scale). Your dashboard: Overview, Projects, Unit tracker, Construction, Review status, Agreement, Pipeline, Finance, "+ New project". Site Intelligence and "Submit a Site" are linked.

## What you can do now
1. **Submit a project** with units, pricing, images and documents. Nothing is public until MANOP reviews it.
2. **Follow Review status.** MANOP may approve, flag, or ask for information; reply by updating the project.
3. **Post updates and track units and construction.** Updates are labelled by who posted them and whether MANOP verified them.
4. **Pipeline.** Introductions MANOP has referred to you appear here. Held introductions are invisible to you until referred. You see the buyer's request (unit, price at the time, purpose), not MANOP's fee fields.
5. **Deal Room.** Acknowledge receipt, message the buyer and MANOP, report or confirm milestones. Acknowledging receipt does not make you the payer of any fee.
6. **Finance tab.** Instalment-plan requests from buyers on your developments.
7. **Agreement tab.** Request a commercial agreement with MANOP. Terms are a versioned record; the proposed developer schedule (2% up to NGN 500m, 1.5% up to 1bn, 1% above, on the whole value) is NOT approved until an admin approves a terms version. A fee is only ever calculated from an executed agreement and is non-binding until MANOP confirms.

## What admin does for you
Reviews and publishes your projects, verifies your account, holds and refers introductions, resolves milestone disputes, approves terms, records agreement execution and any fee decision.

## Not available
No automatic commission, no payment processing, no landowner-style single-site screen.
