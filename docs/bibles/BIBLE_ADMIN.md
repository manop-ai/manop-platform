# MANOP admin guide

## Capacity
Admin rights come from the database (is_manop_admin), never from editable profile data. /admin groups work as:
- **Intelligence:** Command Center, Activity, Signal Monitor, Research, Agreement Requests.
- **Operations:** Verify Queue, Introductions (/admin/leads), Financing, Finance Intelligence, Lender Products, Agencies, Associations, Onboard.
- **Reviewed Developments:** add/manage, Review Submissions, Comparables.
- **Site Intelligence:** Site Review Queue.
- **Commercial:** /admin/commercial (terms versions, subscriptions, agency routes).

## Daily routine
1. Verify Queue and Review Submissions: approve, flag, or request information.
2. Introductions: hold or refer; read Deal Room conversations (you receive an email when a party writes); leave internal notes (checkbox, hidden from parties).
3. Financing: read requests, use the handover panel, record outcomes; keep Lender Products current.
4. Commercial: approve terms only after the founder decides figures, entitlements and legal review; record payment references; activate routes.

## Rules you hold
No "approved/pre-qualified/best/recommended" language on lender outputs. Subscription is not verification. No automatic commission; a fee figure is an indicator until MANOP confirms. Do not authorise from user-editable metadata.
