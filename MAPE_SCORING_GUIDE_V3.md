# MAPE Scoring System V3 — Complete Reference Guide

## Overview

**MAPE** = **M**anop **A**gency **P**erformance **E**ngine

The MAPE system is Manop's reputation and trust framework. It scores agencies from 0–1000 points across 5 activity-based dimensions. All points must be **EARNED through activity** — there are **no free points for signing up**.

**Total Points:** 1000 (always)  
**Scoring Model:** Activity-weighted (V3 redesigned)  
**Philosophy:** Reputation is built, not granted

---

## Point Structure (1000 Total)

| Dimension | Points | Weight | What It Measures | Starting Score |
|-----------|--------|--------|------------------|-----------------|
| **I** — Intelligence | 0–350 | 35% | Verified transactions submitted | 0 |
| **P** — Performance | 0–250 | 25% | Real buyer interactions & deals | 0 |
| **M** — Market Quality | 0–200 | 20% | Professional listing standards | 0 |
| **A** — Activity | 0–100 | 10% | Platform engagement (reduced from 120) | 0 |
| **E** — Ethics | 0–100 | 10% | Integrity & transparency (redesigned) | 0 |

**Every new agency starts at 0 points in all dimensions.**

## Dimension Breakdown & How to Earn Points

### I — Intelligence (0–350 pts) | 35% of Total

**What It Measures:** Verified transaction data submitted to Manop  
**Why It Matters:** Market transactions are Manop's core data asset. Agencies that submit verified sales give Manop competitive advantage and neighborhood intelligence.  
**Key Rule:** Cannot be faked — requires Manop or cross-agency verification  

#### How Points Are Earned:

| Component | Points | How to Earn | Cap |
|-----------|--------|------------|-----|
| Transaction Volume | 20 pts/transaction | Submit + pass Manop verification | 280 pts (14 txns) |
| Market Breadth | 10 pts/neighborhood | Cover different neighborhoods with transactions | 70 pts (7 neighborhoods) |

**Example Progression:**
- 0 transactions = **0 pts**
- 1 verified transaction = **20 pts**
- 5 verified transactions = **100 pts**
- 10 verified transactions (3 neighborhoods) = **230 pts**
- 14+ verified transactions (7+ neighborhoods) = **350 pts** ✓ FULL CREDIT

**Tips for Agencies:**
- Submit your first transaction — single highest-impact action
- Include proof: property deed, bank statement, buyer agency confirmation
- Cover different neighborhoods to maximize breadth bonus
- Each verified transaction strengthens your profile AND Manop's data

---

### P — Performance (0–250 pts) | 25% of Total

**What It Measures:** Real buyer interactions leading to deals  
**Why It Matters:** Proof that your listings actually attract buyers, and you can close sales  
**Key Rule:** ONLY grows when buyers contact you through Manop platform  

#### How Points Are Earned:

| Component | Points | How to Earn | Notes |
|-----------|--------|------------|-------|
| Reply Rate | 100 pts | Reply to 100% of inquiries | Each missed inquiry = lost points |
| Lead Advancement | 100 pts | Move leads to viewing/negotiation/offer/closed stages | Requires pipeline management |
| Deal Closure | 50 pts | 1 pt/closed deal, cap 50 | 5 closed deals = full |

**Example Progression:**
- 0 inquiries received = **0 pts** (waiting for buyers)
- 10 inquiries, reply to all, no advancement = **100 pts** (good responsiveness)
- 10 inquiries, 80% reply, 60% moved to viewing = **100 + 60 = 160 pts**
- 10 inquiries, 100% reply, 100% advanced, 3 closed = **100 + 100 + 30 = 230 pts**
- 10 inquiries, 100% reply, 100% advanced, 5+ closed = **100 + 100 + 50 = 250 pts** ✓ FULL CREDIT

**Tips for Agencies:**
- Respond to ALL inquiries immediately (even to say you don't have the property)
- Log viewings and keep pipeline up-to-date
- Mark deals as closed when transaction completes
- Performance only measures buyer interactions through Manop — do deals offline? Log them

---

### M — Market Quality (0–200 pts) | 20% of Total

**What It Measures:** Professional presentation of listings  
**Why It Matters:** Affects user experience, buyer confidence, and platform credibility  
**Key Rule:** Points awarded as PERCENTAGES of your total listings (consistency matters)  

#### How Points Are Earned:

| Component | Points | How to Earn | Progress |
|-----------|--------|------------|----------|
| Images | 60 pts | 70%+ of listings have ≥3 photos | Partial credit at lower %s |
| Data Completeness | 60 pts | Avg of price%, bedrooms%, description% ≥ 100% | Each field = 20 pts |
| Title Documentation | 40 pts | 100% of listings specify title doc type | 50% → 20 pts, 100% → 40 pts |
| Freshness | 60 pts | 50%+ listings updated in last 30 days | Monthly updates required |

**Example Progression:**
- 10 listings, 3 with photos, no descriptions, no title docs, never updated = **M score:**
  - Images: (3/10 ÷ 0.7) × 60 = ~25 pts
  - Data: (1 + 0 + 0) ÷ 3 × 60 = 20 pts
  - Title: 0 pts
  - Freshness: 0 pts
  - **Total M = ~45 pts** (needs work)

- 10 listings, 8 with 3+ photos, all priced/beds/desc filled, 8 with title docs, 6 updated = **M score:**
  - Images: (8/10 ÷ 0.7) × 60 = ~57 pts ✓ near max
  - Data: (1 + 1 + 1) ÷ 3 × 60 = 60 pts ✓ FULL
  - Title: (8/10) × 40 = 32 pts
  - Freshness: (6/10 ÷ 0.5) × 60 = 60 pts ✓ FULL
  - **Total M = ~209 pts** (capped at 200) ✓ EXCELLENT

**Tips for Agencies:**
- Add 3+ professional photos to EVERY listing (photos get 3× more views)
- Fill price, bedrooms, description on all listings
- Specify title document type (C of O, Governor's Consent, Freehold, etc.)
- Update listings monthly: mark sold, adjust price, refresh photos
- Quality >> Quantity (10 professional listings beat 30 neglected ones)

---

### A — Activity (0–100 pts) | 10% of Total

**What It Measures:** Platform engagement and usage frequency  
**Why It Matters:** Active agencies are trustworthy, responsive, and more likely to close deals  
**Key Rule:** REDUCED from 120 to 100 (lower weight than transaction/performance/quality)  

#### How Points Are Earned:

| Component | Points | How to Earn | Notes |
|-----------|--------|------------|-------|
| Login Frequency | 50 pts | 15+ logins in last 30 days | ~Every 2 days |
| Login Recency | 10 pts | Logged in within last 7 days | Only if consistent |
| Listing Updates | 40 pts | Any listing updated in last 30 days | Mark sold, refresh price |
| Inactivity Decay | −2% per 90 days of no login | Applies to P+M+A+E only, not I | Capped at −20% |

**Example Progression:**
- 0 logins in 30d, no updates = **0 pts** (dormant account)
- 5 logins, no recent activity, no updates = **~17 pts** (some engagement)
- 15+ logins, logged in within 7d, 3+ listings updated = **50 + 10 + 40 = 100 pts** ✓ FULL CREDIT

**Tips for Agencies:**
- Set up mobile notifications so you see inquiries immediately
- Log in 2–3× per week (every other day target)
- Update listings monthly: adjust prices, mark sold, refresh photos
- Activity shows you're actively serving buyers

---

### E — Ethics (0–100 pts) | 10% of Total

**What It Measures:** Integrity, transparency, and compliance  
**Why It Matters:** Trust foundation. No reputation without clean ethics  
**Key Rule:** COMPLETELY REDESIGNED — **ALL points require activity** (no free points for "not being bad")  

#### How Points Are Earned:

| Component | Points | How to Earn | Notes |
|-----------|--------|------------|-------|
| Transaction Activity | +20 | ≥1 verified transaction submitted | Proves you're real in market |
| Verification Docs | +15 | Legal approval from Manop (CAC verified) | Available after legal review |
| Association Membership | +10 | Active membership in recognised real estate association | Extra trust for local industry collaboration |
| Fee Transparency | +15 | 50%+ listings disclose agency fee | Partial credit at lower %s |
| Title Transparency | +15 | 50%+ listings specify title doc type | Same as M, but ethics context |
| Clean Record | +15 | Zero complaints in 180 days | −20 pts per complaint |
| No Fake Flags | +10 | Zero fake listing flags | −25 pts per flag |
| Professionalism | +10 | Admin-assigned rating (0–5) scaled to 0–10 | Manual only |
| **Penalty** | −20/each | Per complaint (cumulative) | Can push E negative, floored at 0 |

**Example Progression:**
- Brand new agency (0 transactions, no verification) = **0 pts** (nothing earned yet)
- 1 transaction verified + verification pending = **20 pts**
- 1 transaction + verified docs + 50% fee disclosure + 0 complaints = **20 + 15 + 15 + 15 = 65 pts**
- 3+ transactions + verified + fees disclosed + title docs disclosed + clean record = **20 + 15 + 15 + 15 + 15 + 10 + 10 = 100 pts** ✓ FULL CREDIT

**Tips for Agencies:**
- First step: submit a transaction (unlocks +20 ethics points)
- Second: get verified (legal documents → +15 ethics points)
- Third: join a recognised industry association for additional trust points
- Fourth: disclose fees & title docs on listings (+30 ethics points)
- Keep record clean: resolve complaints quickly, don't list fake properties
- Transparency builds trust, trust builds reputation

## Badge System & Qualification Gates

Badges are **NOT** bought with points alone — they require **specific activity gates**.

### Badge Thresholds

| Badge | Total Score | Appearance | What It Signals |
|-------|-------------|-----------|-----------------|
| **Listed** | 0–399 | ○ Gray circle | New account, just starting |
| **Verified** | 400–599 | ◇ Blue diamond | Approved + active |
| **Trust** | 600–799 | ◈ Teal crystal | Proven delivery + deals |
| **Elite** | 800–1000 | ◆ Gold diamond | Top tier, exceptional |

### Badge Qualification Gates

To display a badge, **score alone is not enough**. You must meet dimension-specific gates:

#### Listed (0–399)
- **Gate:** None (default state)
- **Meaning:** Just signed up, earning points

#### Verified (400+)
- **Gate 1:** I ≥ 40 OR M ≥ 120 (transactions OR quality)
- **Gate 2:** E ≥ 30 (minimum ethics)
- **Gate 3:** Must be verified by Manop legal team or CAC approval
- **Meaning:** You're legally approved and actively participating

**Example:** 
- 2 verified transactions (I=40) + quality listings (M=150) + fees disclosed (E=35) + verified docs = **Verified badge** ✓

#### Trust (600+)
- **Gate 1:** I ≥ 150 (8+ verified transactions minimum)
- **Gate 2:** E ≥ 50 (strong ethics)
- **Gate 3:** P ≥ 50 (some deal activity)
- **Meaning:** You've proven consistency and delivered real deals

**Example:**
- 8 transactions (I=160) + replied to all, closed 2 deals (P=120) + clean record (E=55) = **Trust badge** ✓

#### Elite (800+)
- **Gate 1:** I ≥ 280 (14+ verified transactions)
- **Gate 2:** M ≥ 180 (professional listings across portfolio)
- **Gate 3:** E ≥ 80 (impeccable record)
- **Gate 4:** P ≥ 100 (strong closing rate)
- **Meaning:** Exceptional across all dimensions, top-tier agency

**Example:**
- 15 transactions (I=300) + strong leads (P=150) + excellent listings (M=190) + verified + transparency (E=85) = **Elite badge** ✓

### Why These Gates?

- **Prevents gaming:** Can't reach Trust by submitting 50 fake photos without transactions
- **Ensures real activity:** Elite requires transactions + market data + buyer trust + professionalism
- **Aligns with value:** Badges match what buyers see on your profile

## Critical Rules & Gotchas

### Rule 1: All points earned through activity, zero points for signup
- New agency starts at 0, not 100
- Verification does NOT grant points automatically (just gates badges)
- Fee disclosure only counts if fees object populated in listing form

### Rule 2: Performance = only real buyer interactions
- Inquiries MUST come from Manop (not your CRM)
- Status must be logged as: new → replied → viewing → negotiation → offer → closed
- Offline deals: manually log as inquiries to count

### Rule 3: Market Quality requires consistency across portfolio
- One perfect listing doesn't help if 9 others are abandoned
- Percentages matter: 50% photos, 100% price, 80% description = ~70 pts, not capped
- Updates drop off points over time (30-day window)

### Rule 4: Ethics gates require verification + activity
- Verification alone = 15 points (no badge until other gates met)
- Fees/title transparency counted only if listings exist
- Complaints penalize immediately: 1 complaint = −20 E points

### Rule 5: Badges are gated, not just thresholds
- Having 400 points ≠ Verified badge (need gates: I or M, E, verification)
- Having 600 points ≠ Trust badge (need gates: I, P, E)
- You can have high total score but lower badge if gates aren't met

## Cron Schedule & Updates

- **Frequency:** Every 6 hours
- **Timezone:** UTC
- **Computation Time:** ~5 minutes for all agencies
- **Data Freshness:**
  - Transactions: 90-day window
  - Inquiries: all-time (but scored on recent activity)
  - Listings: realtime (updated_at checked)
  - Activity log: 30–180 day windows
  - Complaints: 180-day window
- **System status:** Keep cron health as an internal operational signal only, not as user activity monitoring.
